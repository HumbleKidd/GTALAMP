Shipping and returns

What happens after you pay.

Shipping

  • Every order ships from the United States.
  • We pack it after the payment screenshot matches the order.
  • US orders usually hand off in 1 to 3 business days. Most arrive in about a week.
  • Other countries ship from the same desk. Any duty is billed by the carrier, not by us.
  • Gift box is $25 once and shipping on that order is free.
  • No gift box means shipping is extra. We send the cost and wait for a yes before the label prints.
  • A full street, city, state, and ZIP is required. A bad address that comes back is a reship on the buyer.

Refunds and returns

  • Dead panel, cracked face, or damage in the box: photo it the day it arrives and send it with the payment screenshot. We replace it or refund it. You do not pay return shipping unless we ask for the lamp back.
  • Changed your mind: tell us within 14 days of delivery. Unused, in the original box. You pay the return shipping. Refund goes back to the same payment after it reaches us.
  • No refund if it was dropped after delivery or returned without the panel and cable.
  • Fan-made lamp, not a Rockstar product. “Not official” is not a return reason.
